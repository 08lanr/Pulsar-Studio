# Metric dictionary — Title Analytics

Generated from `lib/analytics/definitions.ts` (the UI reads the same list; every displayed metric carries a `definition_key` from it). Source research: `docs/analytics/tiktok-source-mapping.md`, accessed 2026-09-08. Analytics version 1.0.

**Provenance classes.** `documented` = named and defined in TikTok's mini-drama analytics documentation (app level); `calculated` = Pulsar derives it from documented inputs with the formula shown; `proposed` = we want it at this granularity but no public source verifies that a provider exposes it. **Evidence labels** follow the market desk (`observed | inferred | estimated | partner_reported`): a value the platform reports is partner-reported, a Pulsar formula over such values is inferred, an assumption (the fee share, the demo dedupe model) is estimated, a campaign result row is observed. In fixture mode every value additionally carries `source: "demo"` and the page shows the "Demo analytics" chip.

## Conventions that hold on every view

- **Period.** The selected range (7d / 30d / 90d, `?range=`) ends at the listing's last data day (`data_through`), in UTC, in USD. The freshness line shows the lag to the demo clock (2026-09-08). A range with fewer covered days than its length is *partial*; a lag over 7 days is *stale*.
- **Previous-period comparison** exists only when the current and the immediately preceding period are both fully covered. Counts and amounts change in **%**; rates change in **percentage points (pp)**; the two are distinct types (`Comparison.kind`).
- **Missing is null.** An unobserved or undelivered value is `unavailable` with a reason, never 0. A step that cannot apply (a free episode's unlock rate) is `not_applicable`.
- **Rates carry their numerator and denominator** (`RateMetric`), and the UI prints them under the value.
- **Unique users are never summed across days.** Period-unique viewers = new viewers in the period + the deduplicated share of returning viewer-days (dataset `dedupe.viewers`, 0.35 in the demo); period paying users = new payers + the deduplicated share of returning payer-days (`dedupe.payers`, 0.45). Daily viewers appear only in the chart and its table. A real connector must deliver period-unique figures directly; the dedupe model is a demo stand-in and is labelled estimated.
- **Recharge vs redemption.** Buying coins (recharge orders, GMV) is money; spending coins (redeem orders, coins) is not. They are shown side by side and never added.
- **Waterfall.** IAP gross − refunds − fees/share = publisher earnings; settled covers days the platform has settled (T+1, demo cutoff 2 days); paid out is a separate field and unavailable from this source. Ad revenue is reported after the platform's fee and is kept outside the IAP waterfall.
- **Episode revenue** is the redeemed recharge value mapped to unlocks of that episode; the sum over episodes is at most the title's IAP gross (coins bought but not redeemed stay unmapped). The Episodes view prints both figures.
- **Attribution.** Campaign metrics are built from the existing `promote.results` rows (never re-derived); attributed users/payers/revenue are reported by the ad platform (demo: stored per campaign); attributed revenue is at most the title's IAP gross in the campaign window; the remainder is *unattributed*, never "organic". Cohort ROAS = attributed gross sales / spend and is not profit; it is shown only when attributed revenue exists. Ad impressions are never added to title viewers.
- **Cohorts.** Entry = first start of any episode of the listing on that day. Observed D7/D30 = eligible cumulative cohort revenue / initial eligible cohort users, over the latest `range` entry days whose horizon has elapsed (entry window ends at `data_through − horizon`). Immature when no entry day has elapsed. Gross basis. The platform's own LTV ("average sales for new users in the date range") is shown apart and never mixed in. No lifetime forecast.
- **Continuation** N→N+1 = starters of N who started N+1 within 3 days / eligible starters of N (started at least 3 days before `data_through`). Mid-title entries into N+1 are not in the numerator; replays are not counted twice; watching N+1 before N does not count; the last episode with data has no continuation. Unique starting viewers on the Episodes view are daily-unique summed over the period (stated on the page). Rows under 100 unique starters are marked small sample and other flags are suppressed.
- **Funnel** steps are event counts (entries, playbacks, last-free-episode views, unlock interactions) and orders (successful unlocks) and people (continued after unlock); the page says they are not one linked user cohort. The largest ratio drop is marked with text.
- **Takeaways** are generated by fixed rules from displayed numbers (largest continuation loss under 50%, payer-conversion movement ≥ 0.5 pp, revenue mix ≤ 60% / ≥ 85% IAP, largest funnel loss, attribution boundary or no campaign, coverage) and describe; they never state a cause.

| `iap_share` | IAP share of revenue / 内购占比 | rate | title | selected period | IAP gross + ad revenue | calculated | inferred | iap_gross / (iap_gross + ad_revenue). Unavailable when either side is unavailable. |

| `payer_conversion` | Payer conversion / 付费转化率 | rate | title | selected period | viewers (period-unique) | calculated | inferred | paying_users / viewers. TikTok's 'percentage of paying users' uses active users of the app as denominator; ours uses viewers of the listing. |
| `repeat_payers` | Repeat paying users / 复购用户 | people | title | selected period | — | documented | partner_reported | Deduplicated users with more than one successful recharge order in the period. |

| `arppu` | ARPPU / 付费用户平均收入 | usd_per_user | title | selected period | paying users | documented | inferred | iap_gross / paying_users (gross basis). |

| `cohort_d30` | Observed D30 revenue per user / D30 观测人均收入 | usd_per_user | cohort | 30 days after cohort entry | initial eligible cohort users | calculated | inferred | eligible cumulative cohort revenue / initial eligible cohort users. Eligible = entry date at least 30 days before data end. |
| `paywall_reached_ep` | Reached paywall / 到达付费点 | people | episode | selected period | — | proposed | partner_reported | Unique starters of the episode who saw the unlock prompt for the next episode. |
| `paid_unlocks` | Paid unlocks / 付费解锁 | orders | episode | selected period | — | proposed | partner_reported | Redeem orders that unlocked this episode. |

| `unlock_conversion` | Unlock conversion / 解锁转化率 | rate | episode | selected period | unlock interactions on this episode | calculated | inferred | (paid_unlocks + ad_unlocks) / unlock_attempts. |
| `episode_revenue` | Directly mapped episode revenue / 直接归属剧集收入 | usd | episode | selected period | — | proposed | estimated | Recharge value redeemed on this episode's unlocks. The sum over episodes is at most the title's IAP gross; coins bought but not yet redeemed stay unmapped. |
