# Pulsar Studio engineering guide

This is the working contract for repository changes. Product scope and vocabulary live in `PRODUCT.md`; newer entries at the top of `docs/decisions.md` override older prose.

## Current product boundary

- **Unified launch supersedes the older Promote creation journey (2026-09-16 acceptance):** `docs/unified-launch.md` records the user's final scope. One episode cutter feeds Clips. TikTok download/post/Spark-code generation are manual; paste codes in Launch. Meta supports both existing posts and finished files. Multi-account preview and immutable approver submission feed the shared launch sweep and Monitor. Staff administrators may launch on behalf with an authorization note. Budget is a total lifetime amount per provider run, allocated into signed per-campaign ceilings; daily pacing is separate. Higher ceilings require a new round and approval. Old creation, approval, submit, retry and relaunch paths are retired; Earlier campaigns retains read-only records/results and stop controls. Old scheduling is review/metrics only unless `LEGACY_PROMO_RECOVERY=1` explicitly enables temporary recovery. Older bullets below describe historical behavior where they conflict. Revenue, Pixel and Conversions API are deferred; ask Ruobin before that later phase. See `docs/launch-acceptance-2026-09-16.md` for verification and live limitations.

- **Studio is the market desk (2026-09-06 pivot, top of `docs/decisions.md`):** the producer portal opens on a market overview (public charts from ReelShort and DramaBox run through one trope taxonomy), then the producer's own catalog against that market, then hooks and promotion. Adapt and Promote stay as tools under "My titles"; Adapt is frozen (no new pipeline work). Promote launches on TikTok from Studio itself (decision 2026-09-09, top of `docs/decisions.md`); the sibling Grow repository is where the TikTok code was ported from, not a runtime dependency. Do not add Stage.
- **TikTok launch (2026-09-09):** every TikTok call goes through `lib/tiktok/` (`tiktokTransport()` picks the fake in fixture mode, the live transport in Supabase mode; nothing else may fetch against TikTok). The launch engine (`lib/tiktok/launch.ts`) is idempotent and resumable: one `promote.launches` row per approval manifest, each step's TikTok id recorded before the next step, never a second campaign on retry; the ad group budget is exactly the approved experiment budget. Statuses from `launching` on belong to TikTok's lifecycle and are written only by the engine, the scheduler (`lib/tiktok/scheduler.ts`) and the staff controls; `advancePromoCampaign` is an audited override. Results read back carry `source: "tiktok"` (fixture read-back: `demo`), hook hold is the 2-second watch rate, landing actions stay null. Only a staff-assigned, connected ad account (`assigned_by` set) is a launch account. Tokens live in `.tokens.json` / `.env.local`, never in the data layer, a fixture seed or a response. Fixture mode never reaches a real TikTok; real launches need `DATA_SOURCE=supabase` (the `studio-live` launch config).
- **Launch settings, controls and the monitor (2026-09-16, top of `docs/decisions.md`):** a campaign's `launch_settings` (`lib/tiktok/settings.ts`; targeting, budget shape, schedule, bidding, pacing, CTA, launch state, auto-duplicate copies) is snapshotted onto the launch row at submit and validated against the signed budget (`validateLaunchSettings`; gate blocker `settings_invalid`). The signed budget stays the ceiling: lifetime shape = equal shares across every ad group, daily shape = a lifetime cap on the campaign. Staff presets live in `promote.launch_presets`. Post-launch controls go through `lib/tiktok/controls.ts` only (pause/resume, end, budget, daily budget, cost cap, schedule end, duplicate, ad group switch), for staff and the company's approver alike; every control loads the account and ids from our own launch record, reads back, and records via `recordLaunchChange` (a budget change is a new signed experiment version shared across the groups that exist or are still planned; staff changes are audited overrides; both backends write an audit row). A cost-cap replacement carries only the old group's unspent budget and its switch state, and is recorded before the old group is retired; the first switch-on of a paused launch enables its groups (`activated_at`). Creation is crash-safe by name adoption (unique campaign/ad group/ad names per launch); `lib/tiktok/autodup.ts` re-shares the existing groups and makes the copies once ads clear review; `lib/tiktok/monitor.ts` is the one sweep the desk and the producer page read; `creativeByAdId` maps copy ads back to their creatives for results. Business Centers are linked to companies on `/tiktok`; a producer may name a preferred account inside their BC (SQL trigger `core.guard_company_account_write` keeps everything else on an assigned row staff-only). Relaunch on another account is staff-only, after end/failure, switches the previous campaign off with read-back first, and is the only door out of `ended`.
- **Organic Meta posting (2026-09-16, top of `docs/decisions.md`; plan `docs/meta-organic-plan.md`):** a rendered clip is posted to a company's Facebook Page (a Page video post) or Instagram (a Reel) from Studio, and the resulting `pageID_postID` / media id becomes the ad content. `promote.clip_posts` (migration 0014, CAS `revision`, RLS as 0013) is the **only** source of truth for "posted"; nothing infers it from Meta. One *live* published row per clip × platform × connection (partial unique index `where status = 'published' and superseded_by is null`, mirrored in fixture): "Post again" is a deliberate extra row that persists its own post id, supersedes the row it replaces and only then becomes published — Studio never creates a public post it cannot record, and a late failure keeps the id on the row with a sentence naming it. A staff administrator or the company's approver may post — the same rule as launching; everyone else, viewers included, reads the state. Both backends audit `clip_post_created | published | failed | retried | superseded`. `lib/meta/publish.ts` takes a ten-minute row lease (`lease_owner` / `leased_until`, as on launch runs) and persists `step` before every external call: a lost create response is closed by the stored id, or by adopting a Meta object **only** on a row whose `attempted_at` says it sent that call, whose Facebook video title carries the row's own id, and which no other post row already holds (two matches are reconciled by a person, never guessed). A transient refusal (rate limit, 5xx, timeout, codes 1/2/4/17/32/613) leaves the row `publishing` for the scheduler sweep, a permanent one fails it with the reason. The Page access token is derived per call by `liveMetaTransport.forPage(pageId)` (`GET /{page_id}?fields=access_token`) and is never stored, logged, seeded, returned or put in an error; the fake's `forPage` returns itself. Fixture mode never reaches Meta, a live POST still needs `META_LIVE_WRITES=enabled`, the clip must be `rendered` with a stored SHA-256 and that hash is re-verified on the uploaded bytes, and Instagram's 24-hour publishing quota is read before every Reel. `lib/meta/fake.ts` models the whole chain (`{page}?fields=access_token`, `{page}/videos`, `{video}?fields=post_id`, `{ig}/content_publishing_limit`, `{ig}/media`, `{container}?fields=status_code`, `{ig}/media_publish`, `{media}?fields=permalink`, and the `{page}/posts` / `{ig}/media` listings behind the launch popup's "From the Page" tab); `META_FAKE_PUBLISH=ig_error | fb_throttle` drives its failure paths.
- **Pre-launch review (2026-09-17, top of `docs/decisions.md`):** every new Meta ad set sends `targeting_automation: { advantage_audience: 0 }`; a Meta end time behind the clock is refused at preview (`scheduleEnded`) and again by the driver, and a slipped start begins a minute from now while a sent intent keeps the start it was sent with. A provider's transient refusal (`providerRetryDelay`: Meta codes 1/2/4/17/32/613, 5xx or a lost response; a TikTok timeout, unreachable host or throttle) leaves the campaign `pending` and `waiting` for a 60-second wake-up, at most twelve times (`provider_retries`, reset by Retry), never failed at the first rate limit and never resent blindly. The worker's per-write ownership check reads the Meta inventory through its cache (`getLaunchConnections(…, false)`). A failed launch with no provider campaign reads `failed`, not "not checked". An Instant Page create with a lost answer is settled by `/page/get/` (adopt one match, resend on none, refuse more). A Page with a linked Instagram account is assigned only with that account.
- **Ad angles and auto-cut clips (2026-09-14, top of `docs/decisions.md`):** every ad belongs to an angle in `lib/angles.ts` (direct clips active at $50 minimum per ad; narration registered as coming soon, no engine). After an episode video is stored, `lib/clips/*` cuts 20–30 s 9:16 clips in the background (`scheduleClipCut` from the ingest and replace-video routes; script-ranked via `find_clips`, footage signals as the fallback; clean cuts, nothing burned into the picture, the hook is ad copy only; a landscape source is kept whole over a blurred fill, never zoom-cropped; one `cut_clips` job per episode, refused while one is alive, rendered files never deleted). `generatePromoDrafts` builds one candidate per finished clip and refuses without any (no placeholder ads; `generateAds` starts cutting instead); `appendPromoDraftsFromClips` adds later clips to an open round. The hook is the TikTok ad text (`adTextOf`, 100 chars). The approver cannot freeze a pick whose reservations exceed the experiment budget (`budgetCheck`, both backends; `MIN_BUDGET_USD` is the one floor), live modes approve finished files only, and a chosen ad can be unselected. The launch submits ads per creative and fails with the list when any approved ad is missing; a retry submits only those. The brief shows only the delivery the launch sends (US, website traffic, clicks). A change-request revision keeps the parent's finished file when its clip window is unchanged (copy is ad text, not pixels); a moved window clears the render and the revise route re-cuts it in the background. `PROMO_RENDER=off` disables cutting (tests); `FFMPEG_PATH` names the ffmpeg binary when it is not on PATH.
- Market data lives in `lib/research/*`; runs are immutable under `data/research/runs/<run_id>/`, snapshots under `data/research/snapshots/<run_id>.json`, and `data/research/published.json` is the only pointer the loader follows (`npm run research:crawl` = collect + build + publish; the GitHub Actions workflow `research-crawl.yml` runs it daily at 04:30 UTC and commits the result to `main`, so pull before a local run). A run never overwrites another; publication happens only after zod validation; a failed platform is carried forward as stale, never dropped.
- Every displayed number carries an evidence label (`observed | inferred | estimated | partner_reported`) and, for counters, its source field, unit and read time. Raw views are never compared across platforms; *prominence* is within-platform and is not momentum; unobserved values are null, never zero. Movement needs two published days under the same taxonomy version. Publisher ≠ studio; 女频/男频 is audience positioning; synopsis openings are premise examples, not hooks. Competitor spend and conversion are not shown as facts because no public source exposes them.
- **US launch workspace (2026-09-08):** two sidebar groups (decision 2026-09-08 "status board"): **My titles** = My catalog (the landing page, a status board with one row per title: TikTok status, ad status, US potential, each cell opening its section) · Ad campaigns · TikTok performance; **US market** = US overview · What to make next · Explore listings · Data sources; plus Company & accounts. Market pages carry no in-page area tabs; the sidebar is the only area navigation. Ad numbers live only in Ad campaigns and TikTok numbers only in TikTok performance and the per-title TikTok section; there is no Overview page (`/producer` redirects to the catalog). "US potential" is a versioned 0–100 composite in `lib/research/assessment.ts` shown with every fact, evidence label and points; never call it a probability. Experiments are typed records on `promote.campaigns.experiment`; results carry an explicit `source` (`demo` rows exist only in fixture mode and count at half). Fixture mode seeds `data/fixture/demo-catalog.ts` by default; `FIXTURE_SEED=empty` gives the bare seed (the test runner sets it). Since 2026-09-15 the fixture store persists to `.uploads/fixture-state.json` and keeps every non-demo company across restarts, seed bumps and Reset demo (the demo studio itself always follows the seed); the login card offers one button per company. `FIXTURE_PERSIST=off` for tests and any second server on the same checkout.
- **What to make next (2026-09-08, later):** US market insights = US overview + What to make next (tabs, `components/producer/research/InsightsNav.tsx`). `lib/research/next.ts` decides "fresh" only from observed reasons (platform new flag, New/Newest list, release date ≤ 30 days, first seen by Studio with history); growth needs two published days and stays within a platform; a first-seen listing shows "no baseline", never 0. Story-type shares are shares of the fresh cohort with denominators; make-now briefs are templated and labelled inferred. Row grids that repeat (queues, tables) use fixed action columns, never `auto`, so rows align.
- **Demo journey, e2e and title analytics (2026-09-09, top of `docs/decisions.md`):** the fixture seed is one coherent journey (market signal → title 1 → campaign 1 → demo results → next round); `POST /api/demo/reset` / `npm run demo:reset` / the top-bar chip rebuild it (fixture only). `npm run test:e2e` (Playwright, desktop + presentation viewports) must pass alongside the required checks when producer UI changes. Campaign results are read through `lib/research/results.ts` only. Title analytics (`lib/analytics/*`, `/producer/titles/[id]/analytics/*`) keeps values and provenance separate, never invents zeros, never mixes gross/net bases, and returns `requires_connection` outside fixture mode; the TikTok mapping and metric dictionary live in `docs/analytics/`.
- Every source and metric has a registry entry (`lib/research/registry.ts`) with a status computed from the data; the Data & Sources page renders it and metric labels link there. Watchlists and report imports are company-scoped and editor-only (fixture and Supabase alike, migration 0005). The launch-test shortlist is an explained, versioned hypothesis, never a success probability.
- Two portals share one Next.js app: staff routes under `app/(admin)` and producer routes under `app/(producer)` with `/producer` URLs.
- **Producer-first, subtitles-not-dubbing (2026-09-03/04 pivots, see `docs/decisions.md`):** the work happens in the partner portal — a producer uploads subtitles per episode, presses generate, edits lines on one continuous script sheet (no scene confirms anywhere in the producer flow), and finalizes. Deliverables are the clean English script report and the burned-subtitle video; there is no dubbing. The admin portal is oversight. The portal follows the UI grammar of the consoles producers already use (`docs/ui-research.md`); its look stays on our tokens.
- The legacy creative pack remains Pulsar-internal. Producer-facing Promote has its own `promote.*` records and does not expose or depend on that pack. Adapt still accepts optional video with SRT, VTT, ASS/SSA, or plain text; Promote requires shared episode video but never requires Adapt output.
- **Demo replay:** in fixture mode, first-pass and alternatives replay `data/fixture/canned.ts` through `lib/demo-replay.ts` — deterministic, zero spend, no key. Never wire fixture mode to real model calls (`DEMO_REPLAY=0` is the explicit override). `docs/demo/aizailvtu-demo-ep1.srt` must keep matching the bank.

## Required checks

Run `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, and `npm run build` before considering an integration complete. The app runs on port 3200. Fixture mode is the default and must work without a database or API keys.

## Data and identity

- All application reads and writes go through `getData()` in `lib/data/index.ts`; pages and routes do not select tables directly.
- Keep fixture and Supabase behavior equivalent, especially authorization and state-transition failures.
- UUIDs are internal. UI and exports use external ids with the established prefixes (`ttl_`, `ep_`, `sc_`, `ln_`, `ad_`, `ver_`, `rw_`, `alt_`, `var_`, `clip_`).
- `core.profiles` determines portal access. Producers read only their own producer's titles — and (reviewer/approver roles) EDIT them: create titles under their own company, ingest, run the AI pass, edit lines. Per-title enforcement is `requireTitleEditor` (fixture) / `core.can_edit_title()` (SQL, migration 0002). Viewer-role producers stay read-only. Staff may preview the producer portal but may not act there.
- Producers see their own drafts (they author them). Pulsar's API spend (`cost_cents`, jobs) never reaches a producer session.
- Service-role access is server-only and limited to jobs, ingestion tooling, and storage helpers.

## Approval invariants

- A scene's staff state is only `draft | approved` (a staff-side grouping; the producer flow never surfaces or requires it).
- A version's visible state is `draft | in_review | approved`; `superseded` is bookkeeping only.
- Submission creates and hashes an immutable snapshot. Producer review and exports read frozen snapshots, never mutable draft rows.
- Two paths to `approved`, and they never blur: (1) staff `submitVersion` (freeze + `in_review`) then producer `approveVersion` (or admin on-behalf with evidence); (2) producer-approver `finalizeVersion` — one action that freezes the snapshot, writes per-scene sign-off rows automatically, and approves. Staff are refused by finalize, so every approval records which side made it.
- Finalize gates on content readiness only (every line adapted, non-cut lines non-empty, AI-changed lines carry rationale and back-translation; a line a human authored or edited is its own explanation — `lib/data/views.ts` `adaptedLineIssue` is the one rule, shared by both backends, the summaries and the studio banner) — NO scene-confirm requirement, in fixture and SQL alike. The staff submit path stays strict for every author: staff changes are explained to the producer. The finalize route additionally runs `lib/qc.ts` (`runQc`): QC errors return 409 and block; warnings ship visibly.
- Changes after submission fork a new draft; frozen versions are never edited in place.
- Staff approval on behalf is admin-only and requires an evidence note and audit channel.
- Promote approval follows the same principle with a separate invariant: it freezes exact `pc_` creative versions, media hashes, and copy in a hashed manifest. Handoffs are append-only and idempotent.

## LLM and cost invariants

- Every model call creates a `studio.jobs` row with an idempotency key, input/output, usage, status, and integer `cost_cents`.
- Validate structured model output with zod before data-layer writes.
- `LLM_PROVIDER=anthropic|openai|deepseek` selects the gateway; without that provider's API key (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`), AI actions show an unavailable state while fixture mode continues to work. DeepSeek runs through the OpenAI SDK against `https://api.deepseek.com` in JSON mode with the schema stated in the prompt; zod still validates every result.
- Producer-approved immutable snapshots are the translation-memory source of truth. First-pass prompts may retrieve examples Studio-wide, but this corpus is server-only and must never expose another producer's rows through a route or UI; never create a second mutable copy of approved lines.
- Writing passes get their context through `lib/memory` only, in fixed authority order: approved memory → house exemplars → register guide → CC-CEDICT glosses → Tatoeba near-exact pairs. Knowledge blocks go after the cached system blocks and never into idempotency keys (decisions 2026-09-04, "Training the translator").
- Transcription (decision 2026-09-15, `lib/asr.ts`, docs/transcription.md) fills a script-less episode from its audio — only via the explicit Materials button, never silently, and never replacing existing lines. Provider-based (`STUDIO_ASR_PROVIDER`): `local-whisper` spends nothing and may run in demo mode; `openai` is a real model call — demo replay refuses it and the `transcribe_episode` job carries the audio-minute cost. The machine VTT is stored as the episode's source script (`script_format 'asr'`) and parsed back through `lib/ingest` via `attachIngestToEpisode`, both backends alike (migration 0011).

## UI and localization

- Admin chrome defaults to English and producer chrome to Chinese; the locale cookie wins.
- Use `t()` in server components and `useT()` in client components. Add strings under `locales/_keys/`, then run `node scripts/merge-locales.mjs`.
- Chinese source and English adaptation remain in their content languages independent of chrome locale; retain `lang` attributes or bilingual classes.
- Producer-portal Chinese follows `docs/terminology.md` (decision 2026-09-08 "native Chinese pass"): 你 not 您, 剧集 for a title and 分集 for an episode (never 作品), 实测 / 推断 / 估算 / 合作方上报 for the evidence labels, verb–object buttons, noun-phrase labels, a half-width space between CJK and Latin or digits. New keys are written to that standard; `scripts/zh-native/` reruns the DeepSeek pass when a large batch of copy lands.
- Use existing tokens and classes in `app/globals.css`. Do not introduce a CSS framework or external font host.
- Wrapping discipline (decision 2026-09-08 "final UI/UX pass", `app/polish.css` loads last): a "label →" link gets `&nbsp;→`; action columns in repeating tables are fixed-width and their buttons never wrap; the date range is the `RangeControl` segmented control; use the `text-wrap-style` longhand, never the `text-wrap` shorthand, so deliberate `white-space: nowrap` survives.
- Reserve the gold `.btn-approve` for the partner's primary approval action.

## Routes and exports

- API routes follow: same-origin guard, role check, zod validation, data-layer/job call, JSON response. Use the shared handler and API guard.
- Foreign producer resources behave as not found; never leak their existence.
- Render exports on request from approved, then in-review, then draft state, and identify the source in the header. Do not store generated exports or create export jobs.

Staff handle producer promotion change requests on the admin Promote desk (`/promote`), monitor launched campaigns there (sync, pause/resume, retry), and connect Pulsar's Business Center and assign ad accounts on `/tiktok`. Staff never approve a launch.
